Questions

Before you get started

How do you handle 837 claims and 835 remittances?

We ingest your 837 claim files, validate and submit them, then automatically match incoming 835 remittances back to each claim, flagging denials and underpayments as they happen.

Is this HIPAA compliant?

Yes. All claims and remittance data is processed under HIPAA-ready workflows with role-based access and a full audit trail.

Do you integrate with our clearinghouse or PM system?

We integrate via standard X12 EDI (837/835) with most clearinghouses and practice management systems, no rip-and-replace.

Do you only work on claims and revenue analytics?

No. We're primarily a team of IT experts in the healthcare domain. Revenue Analytics is one product we've built ourselves, alongside custom software for scheduling, patient engagement, care coordination and more, using Next.js, React, C#, Python and AI on RAG, Ollama and vector databases.

How fast can we start?

Most teams are processing live claims within two to three weeks of onboarding.
